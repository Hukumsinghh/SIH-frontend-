# 🎓 CollegeConnect AI

### AI-Powered College Discovery, Admission Guidance & Student Support Platform

CollegeConnect AI is an advanced student-focused application designed to help students navigate one of the most confusing stages of their academic journey — **what to do after Class 12 and which college to choose for higher education**.

After completing Class 12, students often struggle to decide which course to pursue, which college is suitable for them, how the admission process works, and where they can get reliable guidance. CollegeConnect AI addresses these challenges by bringing **college discovery, college information, admission guidance, mentorship, doubt solving, and AI-powered assistance** together in a single platform.

The project was developed as a **team project for the Smart India Hackathon (SIH)**.

---

## 🚀 Problem Statement

For many students, completing Class 12 marks the beginning of a confusing decision-making process.

Students often ask:

* What should I do after Class 12?
* Which course should I choose?
* Which college is best for me?
* What are the available college options?
* How do I compare different colleges?
* What admission options are available?
* Where can I get proper guidance?
* Who can answer my college and career-related doubts?

The required information is often spread across multiple platforms, making the process time-consuming and confusing.

### 💡 Our Solution

**CollegeConnect AI** provides a centralized platform that helps students explore colleges, understand their options, seek mentorship, ask doubts, and interact with an AI assistant.

The goal is to help students move from:

```text
Confusion After Class 12
          ↓
Explore Courses & Colleges
          ↓
Research College Options
          ↓
Get Mentorship & Guidance
          ↓
Ask & Resolve Doubts
          ↓
Use AI Assistance
          ↓
Make an Informed Admission Decision
```
